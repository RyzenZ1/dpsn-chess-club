Our Story

Bringing DPSN’s chess players into one community

Bring the chess players of DPS Noida together — and improve the chess culture of the school.The idea behind the club

The Unofficial DPSN Chess Club was started on June 20, 2026 by Acchintya Sinha, the club’s President, with a simple motive — to bring the chess players of DPS Noida together into a single community and to improve the chess culture of the school.

What began as an idea is now a community of 100+ members who compete, analyse, teach, and grow together. Whether you’ve never moved a pawn or you’re chasing a rating milestone, there’s a place for you here.

From weekly arenas and puzzle challenges to game analysis and a dedicated group for memes and casual games — the club is built to make chess at DPSN bigger, better, and a lot more fun.
